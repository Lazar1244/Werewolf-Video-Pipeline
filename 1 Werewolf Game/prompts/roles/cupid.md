- Cupid — Village team. On Night 1 chooses two players (possibly including Cupid) who become Lovers. If one Lover dies, the other dies of heartbreak at the same moment. If the two Lovers are on different teams, both Lovers (and Cupid, if Cupid is a Lover or on the Village team) switch to team Lovers and can win only together; they are told privately. When both Lovers are dead, a Cupid who is not a Lover returns to the Village team (or the Werewolf team if Cupid has wolf nature).

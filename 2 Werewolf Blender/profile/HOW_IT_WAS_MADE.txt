CHANNEL PROFILE PICTURE - HOW IT WAS MADE (2026-10-20)
======================================================
A brain in the centre with a red hue around it, in the videos' cel-shaded, ink-outlined style (owner's idea, after a
reference picture of a pink brain on a blue disc; ours is our own). A separate scene: nothing else changed.

    blender --background --factory-startup --python profile/make_profile.py   (renders out/brain_natural.png, out/brain_glow.png)
    python profile/finish_profile.py                                           (backgrounds -> out/<n>_<look>_<bg>.png, 800x800)

The brain (make_profile.py)
- Two hemispheres: UV spheres squashed to (0.6, 1.0, 0.72), flattened on the inner side and underneath, the frontal
  lobe a little lower and narrower, 0.315 m either side of the middle (the gap is the fissure). A cerebellum at the
  back underneath (0.52, 0.34, 0.26).
- The folds are procedural (geometry nodes, after a level-3 subdivision): the position, wobbled by a noise texture,
  feeds a Wave texture (spherical rings, scale 3.6, distortion 9); its troughs are the creases (map range 0.04-0.3,
  smoothstep), the rest is pushed out 7 cm along the normals as the rounded folds. The cerebellum gets fine horizontal
  bands instead. The fold amount is stored as the attribute "fold".
- Material: cel-shaded (two light bands), the creases darker from "fold"; in the "glow" look the brain is darker and
  the creases glow red, like circuitry. A black ink outline (an inverted shell, 1.4 cm) as in the videos; it also
  draws the deepest creases as thin lines.
- Six dark cables trail down from underneath: the "AI" hint.
- Light: a warm key from the front left, red spot rims from behind. Rendered at 1600x1600 on a transparent background;
  the camera frames the brain automatically (centred a little high, about 60% of the width), so the round crop never
  cuts it.

The backgrounds (finish_profile.py)
- halo: near-black with a soft red glow behind the brain and a thin red light round its outline.
- disc: a solid red disc (darker toward its rim) behind the brain, dark outside it.
Each is scaled to 800x800 (YouTube's size; it shows profile pictures in a circle). out/preview_round.png shows every
version cropped round, big and at the small sizes YouTube uses (98, 40 and 24 px).

The owner's pick: 1_natural_halo.png.

CHANNEL BANNER (2026-10-20)
---------------------------
"MECHANICAL BRAINS AT PLAY" (the owner's slogan) as a red neon sign on near-black, after the owner's "AI NEVER SLEEPS"
reference; red to match the profile picture.
    python profile/make_banner.py   -> out/banner.png (2560x1440, upload this), out/banner_preview.png (safe areas
                                       drawn: blue = what desktop shows, green = what every device shows),
                                       out/banner_desktop_view.png (as desktop shows it)
- Font: Bahnschrift SemiBold Condensed (comes with Windows; close to the reference's), as large as fits 1406 px wide
  (148 px), centred: inside the 1546x423 box every device shows.
- Neon: a white-hot core (1.0, 0.86, 0.84) on a red tube (1.0, 0.13, 0.09), and four layers of red glow (the letters
  blurred 3, 12, 34 and 90 px, strengths 1.0, 0.85, 0.7, 0.45; the wide ones x1.6), added up like light.

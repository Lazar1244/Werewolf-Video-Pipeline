THUMBNAILS - HOW THEY WERE MADE (Game 1, game 3880b590, 2026-10-19)
=====================================================================

What they are
-------------
1280x720 PNGs in the style of the popular "AIs play Mafia" videos: a giant red 3D title behind a row of six
contestants on a glowing red grid floor in red fog, with a dead body lying in front. The contestants are the video's
own hooded figures (same models as the episode), each with its seat colour on the hood and its lab's real logo glowing
on the chest. Every number below is in the scripts; nothing was painted by hand.

Main pick (owner): 5_questions  -  WEREWOLF + five small white tilted questions.
Alternatives:      1_werewolf, 2_whos_the_wolf (with texts, like the main one), 3_two_are_lying (two pairs of red wolf eyes in the fog),
                   (3_two_are_lying also with texts), 4_blood_moon (a huge red moon behind the row).

Where everything lives
----------------------
2 Werewolf Blender/thumbnail/
    make_thumbnails.py    builds the scene in Blender and renders every version (Game 1/<name>.png; the plain
                          renders that get texts on top go to Game 1/bases/<name>_base.png)
    add_questions.py      draws the small white texts onto Game 1/bases/<name>_base.png -> Game 1/<name>.png
                          (5_questions, 2_whos_the_wolf, 3_two_are_lying)
    rasterize_logos.py    logos/*.svg -> 1024 px transparent PNGs (headless Microsoft Edge)
    prep_logos.py         cleans those PNGs -> logos/png/<lab>.png (what Blender uses)
    logos/                the downloaded SVGs + SOURCES.txt (links, sizes, licences)
    Game 1/               this episode's thumbnails (GAME in make_thumbnails.py / OUT in add_questions.py names
                          the folder: use a new one per game)
    thumbnails.blend      the scene as last rendered (open it to tweak by hand)
It reuses the village kit: village/figures.py (the hooded figures), village/common.py (toon/glow materials, fog,
lights, the ink outline), village/intro_stages.py (glow_finish: soft glow on bright things + vignette).

How to rebuild (from "2 Werewolf Blender")
------------------------------------------
1. Logos (only if the SVGs change). Run this one from PowerShell (the sandboxed bash shell can't start Edge):
       python thumbnail/rasterize_logos.py <temp folder>
       python thumbnail/prep_logos.py <temp folder>
2. Render (about 5 s per version on the RTX 2080):
       "C:\Program Files\Blender Foundation\Blender 5.2\blender.exe" --background --factory-startup --python thumbnail/make_thumbnails.py
   or just some versions:  ... make_thumbnails.py -- 5_questions_base 2_whos_the_wolf_base 3_two_are_lying_base 1_werewolf
3. Texts (all three texted versions):
       python thumbnail/add_questions.py

The scene (make_thumbnails.py, build())
---------------------------------------
- World: flat dark red (0.07, 0.004, 0.006).
- Floor: an 80 m black toon plane; the grid is thin glowing red bars (0.9, 0.08, 0.06; strength 1.3) every 1.2 m
  in both directions, running to the horizon.
- Cast: six standing figures (seats 1-6 = Sol, Fable, Gemini, Grok, Astra, Opus), scale 1.5, in a shallow arc at
  x = -3.6 -2.2 -0.75 0.75 2.2 3.6, each turned slightly toward the camera. Seat colours on the hoods.
- Logos: GPT-6 Sol / GPT-6 Astra -> OpenAI, Claude Fable / Claude Opus -> Claude, Gemini -> Gemini, Grok -> Grok.
  Each is a square decal (0.46 m before the 1.5 figure scale) parented to the figure at chest height
  (local 0.033*x, 0.37, 0.92: nudged inward at the row's edges so the wide lens shows it centred under the hood), image texture -> emission (OpenAI and Grok 1.5, Claude 1.15, Gemini 1.25) mixed with
  transparent by the image's alpha, material render method BLENDED. UVs run along local -x so the logos are not
  mirrored (the figures face the camera).
- The body: a seventh figure (purple scarf) lying across the front at (0.7, -0.45), scale 1.4, in a wet red pool
  (a faint red glow (0.42, 0, 0.008) at 0.75 so it reads on the black floor). The village's body pose lies flat on
  its back, which this low camera sees edge-on, so here it is tipped 22 deg toward the camera (BODY_TIP, about the
  world x axis after its 92 deg yaw); its own small pool is hidden (it would tip up into the air); the X eyes are
  doubled and set 5 cm further apart (so they read as two), and the claw tears made bright red and thicker. grow() scales a mesh about its own centre because those
  parts pivot at the head (scaling the object would push them off the face). Only the thumbnail does this: the
  village's figures.py is unchanged.
- Light: a warm area key from the front (900 W), two hard red spot rims from behind (3500 W each).
- Fog: a red volume (0.7, 0.06, 0.05), thicker near the ground, behind and around the row.
- Camera: at (0, -8.2, 2.4) looking at (0, 2, 2.55), 28 mm lens, slightly below eye level so the cast looms.
- Render: EEVEE, 48 samples, Standard view transform, 1280x720.

The title (why it looks sharp)
------------------------------
The first versions rendered the title inside the scene, so the fog and the glow softened its edges (owner: "foggy
with non clear outlines"). Now each version renders in two passes:
1. The scene without the title, then glow_finish (soft glow + vignette).
2. The title alone: same camera and lights, no fog, transparent background, at 200% size, then scaled down 2x2
   (alpha-weighted) for a clean anti-aliased edge.
Then, in numpy: a dark ink outline (the title's shape grown by ~4 px), a soft red halo behind it (that outline
blurred, 55%), and the title laid on top.
Title: Impact, letter spacing 1.04, extruded 0.32, bevel 0.035, face red glow (1.0, 0.06, 0.035) at 0.9, dark red toon sides, plus a dark
copy 3.5% bigger set just behind and down-right as a hard drop shadow. Sizes: WEREWOLF 3.6 (2.9 in the questions
version, raised to z 5.25 to free room), WHO'S THE WOLF? 2.6, 2 ARE LYING 3.0.

The questions (add_questions.py)
--------------------------------
White Impact, black outline (size/12 px), soft dark drop shadow, each tilted a few degrees:
    WHO'S LYING?      left, beside the title         46 px, +8 deg
    CAN AI LIE?       right, beside the title        46 px, -8 deg   (the "AI" hook)
    CLAUDE FABLE      above Fable (seat 2)           40 px, +5 deg   (owner: the best-known models, for those who know)
    GPT ASTRA         above Astra (seat 5)           40 px, -5 deg
    WHO KILLED HIM?   bottom left                    44 px, +5 deg, a curved white arrow (9 px, black edge) to the X eyes
Sizes are set so they still read at 320 px wide (the YouTube home feed).
SPOILERS: check every new game. In this game the wolves were Fable (Claude, seat 2) and Astra (GPT, seat 5). An
early "TRUST CLAUDE?" above Fable was removed for that reason; the CLAUDE FABLE / GPT ASTRA name labels are the
owner's own choice (they name the two best-known models; they also happen to be the two wolves).
WHO'S THE WOLF? gets the same names, WHO KILLED HIM? and arrow, with TRUST NO ONE (left, 42 px, +8) and CAN AI LIE?
(right, 42 px, -8) at y 262, under its wider title.
2 ARE LYING gets the same, with WHICH TWO? on the left (WHO'S LYING? would repeat its title) and the names at
y 266, 38 px, just above the two pairs of wolf eyes.
Change wording or position in LAYOUTS / NAMES / KILL / ARROW in add_questions.py and rerun step 3 (no re-render).

The logos (rasterize_logos.py + prep_logos.py)
----------------------------------------------
Downloaded from Wikimedia Commons (logos/SOURCES.txt), not redrawn. Blender's SVG import can't do Gemini's blurred
colour gradient, so all four are drawn by headless Edge at 1024 px on a transparent background, then:
OpenAI's black mark is recoloured white (it sits on a dark cloak); for Grok only the white mark is kept (the icon's
black rounded square is dropped); Claude (orange) and Gemini (colour gradient) stay as they are. Each is cropped
to its shape and centred on a square canvas.
